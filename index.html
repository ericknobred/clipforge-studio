ClipForge Studio is a desktop application for Windows that helps a creator find, capture, edit and publish short video clips. It runs entirely on the creator's own computer.

YouTube and TikTok integration

After the creator signs in with Google or TikTok, ClipForge Studio can upload the creator's own finished videos to the channel or profile they choose, set the title, caption, description, tags, cover and visibility options, and show the status and basic statistics of those videos. It never posts anything without the creator's explicit action.

ClipForge Studio is a personal tool and is not a service offered to the public.
