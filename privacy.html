Who we are

ClipForge Studio is a desktop application used by its developer on their own computer. There is no ClipForge server and no ClipForge account.

What the app accesses

When you connect a YouTube channel, ClipForge Studio asks Google for permission to (1) upload videos to your channel and (2) read basic information about your channel (name, picture, subscriber and video counts) and about its videos (title, publication date, visibility, view, like and comment counts).

When you connect a TikTok profile, ClipForge Studio asks TikTok for permission to read your basic profile information (display name, avatar, username) and to post videos to your profile on your behalf, only when you press publish.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the videos you choose, to show the channel, profile and the status of your uploads inside the app, and to keep the connection working. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Where the data is stored

The access and refresh tokens issued by Google and TikTok are stored only on your computer, encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI). Channel and video information shown in the app is kept in a local database on your computer. Nothing is sent to servers operated by the developer, and no data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties. Your videos are sent only to the platform you choose.

Google API Services User Data Policy

ClipForge Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

You can disconnect a channel or profile at any time inside the app, which also revokes the authorization. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services (TikTok). Deleting the application's data folder removes everything stored locally.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated.
